# Hi, I'm Nilo Vélez

I've been building websites since 1998 and contributing to WordPress since 2011.

- I moderate the Spanish (Spain) translations of WordPress, and review and translate themes and plugins.
- I'm a team rep for the [Photo Directory](https://wordpress.org/photos/) and part of the WordPress.tv team.
- I'm one of the organizers of the WordPress Sevilla community, Quedadas WordPress Online and WordCamp Europe.

All this community work is possible thanks to the wonderful people at Hostinger, who sponsor a good part of the hours I give to WordPress.

## Things I've made

This account is where I develop most of my projects, along with small tools for the WordPress teams I work with and some personal experiments. Some highlights:

- **[PDF Diva](https://pdfdiva.nilovelez.com/)**: a PDF viewer with presenter view, for everyone who's been told "my slides are a PDF" five minutes before a talk. ([code](https://github.com/nilovelez/pdf-diva))
- **[LadyQR](https://ladyqr.nilovelez.com/)**: QR codes made entirely in your browser, as you type. Nothing is uploaded. ([code](https://github.com/nilovelez/ladyqr))
- **[Machete](https://wordpress.org/plugins/machete/)**: the toolbox I kept rebuilding on every WordPress site, turned into a plugin.
- **[Intentionally Blank](https://wordpress.org/themes/intentionally-blank/)**: a WordPress theme that does as little as possible, on purpose.
